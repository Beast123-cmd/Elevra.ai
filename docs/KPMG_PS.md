# KPMG in India — Smart Campus Analytics: Predict, Optimize & Improve Student Success

Build an Analytics & AI-powered Student Success Platform that can identify students who may require intervention and help college administrators take data-driven actions. Move beyond simply building a dashboard. Use analytics and AI to convert student data into actionable insights and decision intelligence.

## Core Scope
- Data Integration: unified student view from Academic, Attendance, LMS, Engagement, Placement, Skills, Feedback
- Student Success Score from academic + engagement indicators
- At-Risk Identification: poor academic performance; poor placement outcomes
- Interactive Dashboard for faculty/admins: success scores, risk flags, key trends, student insights

Suggested data: Academic (CGPA, marks, backlogs, subject perf); Attendance (overall %, subject-wise); LMS (login freq, assignment completion); Engagement (events, clubs, hackathons, certs); Placement (aptitude, coding, mock interviews); Skills (technical & soft); Feedback (student satisfaction, faculty feedback)

## Bonus
- Student Segmentation (e.g. high academic but low placement readiness)
- Explainable Score (top contributing indicators for each score and risk flag)

## Deliverables
1. Working dashboard prototype on integrated data
2. Short note explaining Success Score (indicators + combination)
3. Short demo or slides

## Evaluation (100)
- Data Integration & Analysis 30% — integration quality across sources, data cleaning, insights from combined data
- Success Score & Risk Identification 25% — relevant indicators, sensible logic, clear at-risk identification (+Segmentation bonus)
- Dashboard & Visualization 25% — interactive, clear, useful for faculty/admins (+Explainable bonus)
- Problem Understanding 10%
- Presentation & Demo 10%
Bonus capped ~5–10 pts. Round 1 also has an AUTOMATED evaluation gate checking mandatory functionality on the deployed link before human judging.
